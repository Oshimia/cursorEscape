## Blocking findings
1. **Promotion retries can overwrite an unresolved recovery journal and erase replacement evidence/backup.** `tools/audio/workflow/promotion.py:110-112` derives a collision-prone journal name from task ordinal plus intended-hash prefix; `tools/audio/workflow/promotion.py:363-365` writes a new `prepared` journal without inspecting an existing one. If a prior attempt reached `destination_replaced`, the retry can overwrite that journal, then `tools/audio/workflow/promotion.py:371-372` overwrite the backup, and `tools/audio/workflow/promotion.py:373-378` record `replacement_occurred: false` because the destination already contains the intended bytes.
   - **Failure scenario:** promotion replaces prior bytes `A` with approved bytes `B`, cache/ledger admission fails, and the journal remains `destination_replaced`. The owner retries `promote_candidate` instead of `recover`. The journal becomes a no-replacement record with `prior_hash = B`, the backup containing `A` is replaced by `B`, and the ledger records a non-replacement even though the actual prior production/staging bytes were `A`. For untracked staging output, rollback evidence is lost.
   - **Required correction:** before creating or replacing a journal, read and validate any existing journal at the same path. Refuse non-`completed` journals and direct the owner through recovery. For `completed` journals, preserve the existing journal/backup and either perform only idempotent cache/ledger reconciliation or verify the ledger evidence before replacing the journal. Add a test that forces a post-replacement admission failure, invokes promotion again, asserts fail-closed preservation of the journal status, original backup bytes, and ledger state, then completes through `recover_promotion`.
